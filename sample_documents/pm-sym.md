# PM-SYM

Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan is a voluntary and contributory pension scheme for eligible unorganised workers. The official portal describes an entry age of 18–40 years and monthly income of ₹15,000 or below. Regular contributors may receive a minimum assured pension of ₹3,000 per month after attaining 60, subject to official conditions and exclusions.

Official source: https://maandhan.in/
